X-ray while reading: four choices, one live prototype

X-ray is Kindle’s panel behind a book: the people, places and terms in it, each with a description and a bar showing where it comes up. The prototype below is the version this library could have. It runs on a real book, The Strange Case of Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde (public domain, from the test fixtures), so the cards say real things and the book keeps a real secret.

The index was written by Claude this session: 20 entries, 112 facts, each with a quote; a script checked every quote word for word against the text, and counts the mentions in each chapter. Nothing here is built in the app. The reader follows your device’s light or dark setting.

Try this first
  1. Leave the choices as they are. In the reader, open Chapter 2 and scroll to its end. Tap Hyde in the text, or in the Who & what list. The card knows only what chapters 1 and 2 have said.
  2. Use the chapter menu to jump to Chapter 10 and tap Hyde again. Now the card ends with the book’s secret.
  3. Go back to Chapter 2 and set choice 4 to C. Tap Hyde. That is what a card that ignores where you are does to a reader.
  4. Switch to Phone and open Study. Then try choice 2 C, which puts the card in the panel instead of over the page.
